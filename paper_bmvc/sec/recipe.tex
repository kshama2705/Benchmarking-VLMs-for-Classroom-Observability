\section{SETA: The Recipe}
\label{sec:recipe}

SETA --- \emph{Subject-aware Engagement via Threshold-tuned
Aggregation} --- is a four-step recipe combining a strong frozen
vision backbone, class-balanced bootstrap aggregation, and val-tuned
ordinal threshold decoding. It is the strongest frozen-feature
DAiSEE engagement recipe we are aware of and runs end-to-end on a
single CPU in approximately thirty minutes.

\subsection{Recipe}

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
  \item \textbf{Features.} SigLIP-L/16-256 CLS-token features at
  $t = 5$~s per clip (1024-d). Available out-of-the-box from
  HuggingFace; no fine-tuning.
  \item \textbf{Bagging.} For $5$ outer seeds
  $\{0, 7, 42, 2025, 1024\}$ and $K = 20$ bootstrap resamples per
  seed, train an LR classifier with class-balanced loss and C-tuned
  on the validation $\kappa_q$ over $C \in \{10^{-3},\dots,10^{2}\}$.
  Average the 100 predicted probability matrices.
  \item \textbf{Expected-class score.} Compute
  $\mathbb{E}[y] = \sum_{k} k \cdot p_k$ on val and test.
  \item \textbf{Ordinal thresholds.} On val, exhaustively search a
  grid of three thresholds $0 \leq t_1 < t_2 < t_3 \leq 3$ in
  steps of $0.04$ and pick the triple maximising val $\kappa_q$.
  Apply the same thresholds on test: $\hat y = k$ iff
  $t_k < \mathbb{E}[y] \leq t_{k+1}$.
  \item \textbf{Report.} Bootstrap 1{,}000 resamples of test
  predictions for 95\% CI.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Statistical significance.} The bootstrap 95\% CI lower
bound for SETA (0.201) exceeds the single-LR argmax baseline mean
(0.199), giving a statistically meaningful improvement. CIs are
computed via 1{,}000 test-set bootstrap resamples.

\paragraph{$K$-fold robustness.} On a 5-fold CV of the validation
split, the threshold-tuned bagged recipe gives
\emph{test} $\kappa_q = 0.226 \pm 0.014$ (mean $\pm$ std across
folds), within bootstrap CI of the full-val number ($0.247$). The
tune--hold gap (val $\kappa_q$ on the tune fold minus the held-out
val $\kappa_q$) is $0.034$, indicating modest---but
non-pathological---val overfit. We recommend reporting both the
full-val and $K$-fold numbers, as we do throughout the paper.

\paragraph{What contributes the gain.} Across all 60+ method variants
we tested (Sec.~\ref{sec:results}), threshold tuning is the single
largest single-intervention at $+0.025$ absolute; bagging adds
$+0.014$; random feature-subspace bagging adds another $\le 0.001$.
Calibration methods (CBU, class-prior~\cite{menon2021logit}) contribute
approximately $0$ once threshold tuning is in place --- the class-
imbalance signal they fix is the same one threshold tuning fixes more
directly. The implication is simple: any reader who wants to deploy a
frozen-feature DAiSEE engagement classifier should start with SETA,
and only invest further effort once it is in place.

\paragraph{Deployment cost.} SETA's full pipeline (100 bootstrap LR
fits + ordinal threshold search + bootstrap CI) runs end-to-end in
under thirty minutes on a single CPU. Inference per clip is one
SigLIP-L forward pass (\textasciitilde 50ms on CPU) plus 100 LR
predictions (negligible) plus threshold application (constant time).
