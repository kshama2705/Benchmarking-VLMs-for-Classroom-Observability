\section{Training details (\textsc{Dream}-FiLM)}
\label{sec:training}

The FiLM variant trains a two-layer MLP (1024 $\to$ 256 $\to$ 4) with FiLM
modulation from the anchor embedding. Class-balanced cross-entropy,
AdamW (lr=$10^{-3}$, weight\_decay=$10^{-4}$), cosine schedule over 40
epochs, batch size 128. Three seeds; final prediction is the mean
softmax across seeds.

\section{ENGAGENET-X (GPU-required supervised baseline)}
\label{sec:engagenet-x-supp}

Section~\ref{sec:engagenet} introduces ENGAGENET-X as the natural
next step: end-to-end SigLIP-L fine-tune with an 8-frame temporal
transformer head, CORN ordinal supervision, focal loss. The training
script is in \texttt{scripts/180\_supervised\_e2e\_gpu.py};
runbook in \texttt{GPU\_RUNBOOK.md}. Expected runtime on a single
A100 is 12-24 hours; published comparable methods (ViBED-Net,
CavT) reach $\kappa_q \approx 0.45$--$0.55$.
